# Art Direction: Guardião da Palavra

- **Style**: Realistic cinematic historical atmosphere.
- **Mood**: Respectful, contemplative, and immersive.
- **Palette**: Warm ambers, deep woody browns, parchment cream, and antique gold accents.
- **Lighting**: Soft candlelight creating warm glows and long shadows.
- **Key Assets**:
    - **Background**: Ancient scribe desk with scattered scrolls, an inkwell, and a soft warm glow.
    - **UI Elements**: Parchment-style windows, wooden buttons, golden seals.
    - **VFX**: Dust motes in the light, subtle candle flicker.
