# Sound Direction: Guardião da Palavra

- **Music**: Ambient, meditative track with ancient harps, soft flutes, and low resonant drones. Instrumental only.
- **SFX**: 
    - **Parchment Unroll**: The sound of dry, thick paper unrolling.
    - **Quill Scratch**: A satisfying soft scratching sound of writing on parchment.
    - **Divine Success**: A soft, resonant golden chime or soft gong.
- **Interaction**: Audio starts on first click. Mute toggle in UI.
