# Art Direction: Mundo Bíblico: Pequenos Heróis

- **Style**: Modern 2D children's book illustration. Clean lines, soft gradients, friendly and inviting.
- **Palette**: Bright and warm. Azure blue, golden yellow, emerald green, coral pink, soft lilac, and pure white.
- **Mood**: Joyful, peaceful, safe, and wonder-filled. No dark corners or scary elements.
- **Subjects**:
    - **Backgrounds**: Lush garden with fruit trees and a rainbow; a warm desert oasis with palms and a clear pond.
    - **Characters**: A friendly, adventurous child hero (wearing simple tunic/modern fusion); a soft white lamb; a majestic but smiling lion.
    - **UI**: A "Passaporte Bíblico" book icon, colorful stickers (star, heart, dove), and the "Sigo com Fé" seal.
- **Perspective**: Side-view or 3/4 top-down for the world; flat for UI elements.
