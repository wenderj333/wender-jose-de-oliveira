# Sound Direction: Mundo Bíblico: Pequenos Heróis

- **Background Music**: Peaceful and uplifting acoustic melody. Focus on gentle guitar, flute, or piano. Instrumental, loopable, and calming.
- **Sound Effects**:
    - **Collection/Success**: A bright, magical "chime" or "sparkle" sound.
    - **Interaction**: A soft, rounded "pop" or "bubble" sound for button presses.
    - **Story Transition**: A gentle "whoosh" or "page turn" sound.
- **Playback**: Audio starts on first interaction. Volume is kept moderate to be child-friendly.
