## Visual Style
- **Style**: Elegant child-style with a soft, magical touch. Similar to high-quality children's book illustrations.
- **Palette**: Gold, Sky Blue, Lilac (soft pastel versions).
- **Lighting**: Soft, ethereal glow with sparkling particles (stars, diamonds).
- **Key Assets**:
  - Three distinct gift boxes: one golden, one sky blue, one lilac. Each with a ribbon and soft shine.
  - Decorative elements: Small floating stars and diamonds for VFX.
  - UI: A light, clean card background with elegant typography.
